sentinel-indices

Ask a question about a place, get an answer from free satellite data: Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery, 245 spectral indices (Awesome Spectral Indices via spyndex), checked against NASA FIRMS fire hotspots. No account, no API key. This page shows precomputed runs and calls no model.

Area: 14 km × 14 km around -2.8, 114.05, Pulang Pisau, Central Kalimantan, during the October 2026 peatland fires. Code: github.com/dzakybd/sentinel-indices · Write-up: dzaky.ai.id

The Deep Agent's answer

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026 (via Earth Search, Element 84) and Landsat 8/9 data (USGS, via Microsoft Planetary Computer). Fire hotspots: NASA FIRMS VIIRS. Burn severity classes: Key & Benson (2006), USGS FIREMON; calibrated on temperate forests, so read them as relative severity on tropical peat. Index changes show that the surface changed, not why or who caused it.
