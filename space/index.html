open-satellite-analysis

Ask a question about a place, get an answer from free public satellite data: Sentinel-2, Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-1 radar, plus NASA fire hotspots. Published spectral indices and simple thresholds. No account, no API key. This page shows precomputed runs and calls no model.
