# Ground-truth references for the satellite case studies

Compiled 2026-10-24. Every "VERIFIED" item was opened and read. Items marked "SNIPPET ONLY" were seen in search results but the page returned HTTP 403, so the wording is not confirmed. Do not quote those without opening them.

Told by Plt Kepala Pelaksana BPBD Pulang Pisau Osa Maliki on 4 Oct 2026, from the Posko Induk Tanggap Darurat Karhutla dan Kekeringan 2026. Same article: highest ISPU 109 (PM2.5, "Tidak Sehat") at Desa Bahaur Beras, Kahayan Kuala. Berita Sampit, 4 Oct 2026. https://beritasampit.com/2026/10/04/karhutla-pulang-pisau-tembus-tujuh-ribu-hektare-lebih-terluas-di-wilayah-jabiren-raya/ (VERIFIED)
- Earlier regency figure: 2,469.8 ha and ISPU 153 ("tidak sehat") as of Wed 9 Sep 2026, 20.00 WIB, per Posko Siaga Karhutla Pulang Pisau. Berita Sampit, 9 Sep 2026. https://beritasampit.com/2026/09/09/karhutla-pulpis-tembus-2-469-hektare-udara-masuk-kategori-tidak-sehat/ (VERIFIED). Growth 2,469.8 to 7,083.6 ha in 25 days.
- Emergency status: Governor Agustiar Sabran said on 30 Jul 2026 that Kalteng had set "Status Siaga Darurat Bencana Karhutla hingga 31 Oktober 2026". RRI, 31 Jul 2026. https://rri.co.id/palangkaraya/info-pemda/2617616/gubernur-kalteng-minta-daerah-percepat-antisipasi-karhutla (VERIFIED). Later: provincial "tanggap darurat" from 31 Aug to 29 Sep 2026. Afu.id, 1 Sep 2026. https://afu.id/nusantara/jeriken-bensin-ditemukan-di-titik-karhutla-kalimantan-bupati-pulang-pisau-sengaja-dibakar (VERIFIED). - BNPB (Abdul Muhari, Pusdatinkom), 31 Jul 2026: Pulang Pisau and Palangka Raya are priority areas, priority point Desa Tumbang Nusa; BNPB data gave 3,069.52 ha burned province-wide. Metro TV link above; same figure in Gebrak.id, 1 Aug 2026 (thick smoke on Trans Kalimantan road in Pulang Pisau cutting visibility). https://www.gebrak.id/2026/08/karhutla-kalimantan-tengah-belum-terkendali-asap-tebal-ganggu-pengendara-dan-luas-kebakaran-tembus-3-069-hektare.html (VERIFIED)

No haze values. Journalarta, 20 Oct 2026. https://journalarta.com/news/2026/10/20/kepala-bnpb-dan-kasad-tinjau-langsung-penanganan-karhutla-di-kalteng-ini-fokus-utamanya/ (VERIFIED)
- El Nino context: BMKG, 10 Jun 2026, says El Nino persists into early 2027 and August 2026 is the widest dry-season peak (48.84% of land area, most of Kalimantan). https://www.bmkg.go.id/berita/utama/bmkg-puncak-musim-kemarau-agustus-2026-perkuat-kesiapan-hadapi-dampak-el-nino (VERIFIED)

The 20 Oct BNPB numbers do not refer to Pulang Pisau. Hotspot counts: RRI-based search summary gave 62 hotspots in Pulang Pisau on 30 Jul, but the RRI page I opened did not contain it, so treat that as unconfirmed. Not found: SiPongi or BMKG regency-level figures.

## 2. Weda Bay Industrial Park (IWIP) and nickel mining, Central Halmahera

- At least 5,331 ha of forest cleared inside nickel mining concessions on Halmahera, about 2.04 Mt CO2e. Climate Rights International, "Nickel Unearthed", Jan 2024 (launched 17 Jan 2024). https://cri.org/reports/nickel-unearthed/ (VERIFIED, first 100k characters; period not stated there, method says Global Forest Watch annual loss).
- Same figure reported as "more than 5,300 ha ... within the park's concession since 2018", geospatial analysis by CRI and UC Berkeley. Reuters via Jakarta Post, 18 Jan 2024. https://www.thejakartapost.com/indonesia/2024/01/18/foreign-backed-nickel-hub-in-indonesia-causing-mass-deforestation--report (VERIFIED)
- Mongabay (Hans Nicholas Jong, 26 Feb 2024): 5,331 ha within nickel concessions on Halmahera; largest single loss PT Weda Bay Nickel 1,456 ha as of 2022; IWIP estate about 5,000 ha, WBN concession 45,065 ha. https://news.mongabay.com/2024/02/indonesian-nickel-project-harms-environment-and-human-rights-report-says/ (VERIFIED)
- Academic: Sabaruddin et al., Jurnal Wasian 11(1), 30 Jun 2024: Central Halmahera loss averaged 1,435.43 ha/yr in 2001-2013; Weda and Maba most affected 2014-2017. https://pub.kehutanan.unismuh.ac.id/index.php/wasian/article/view/128 (VERIFIED, abstract only)

Comparability: The 5,331 ha is concession-wide on Halmahera (Mongabay, CRI) or IWIP-concession-wide since 2018 (Reuters); the two framings conflict, and WBN alone is 1,456 ha to 2022. All are far larger than a Lelilef Sawai box and none is year-by-year, so they cannot validate a specific 2019-2026 interval. A CRI June 2025 follow-up exists (only seen via BHRRC, which returned 403); I could not confirm that it contains an updated hectare figure. Expansion dates by year were not found in any source.

## 3. Ibu Kota Nusantara, KIPP, Sepaku

- KIPP is 6,671 ha in three clusters, to be worked on "this year [2022] through 2024". Minister of PUPR Basuki Hadimuljono, iNews, 23 Feb 2022. https://www.inews.id/finance/bisnis/kawasan-inti-ikn-seluas-6671-ha-terbagi-menjadi-3-klaster/all (VERIFIED; 921 ha not mentioned)
- KIPP about 6,671 ha, mostly on the PT ITCI Hutani Manunggal concession, Sepaku. Antara Kaltim, 23 Aug 2022. https://kaltim.antaranews.com/berita/166081/pemerintah-pusat-diharapkan-beri-kompensasi-lahan-warga-masuk-kipp-ikn (VERIFIED)
- 1,760 ha (26%) of KIPP is the development area; initial land preparation focused on 921 ha in zone A1; progress "baru sekitar 15 persen" (Rozali Indra Saputra, Balai Prasarana Permukiman Wilayah Kaltim). JPNN, 4 Nov 2022. https://m.jpnn.com/news/progres-pekerjaan-di-kawasan-inti-ikn-nusantara-sudah-15-persen (VERIFIED)
- Otorita IKN (Myrna Safitri) says the cleared land was eucalyptus and oil palm plantation, 62% of KIPP is green/protected; NASA published images 22 Feb 2024 (Apr 2022 vs Feb 2024); FWI counted 1,663 ha deforestation 2022 to Jun 2023. Kaltimkece, 12 Mar 2024. https://kaltimkece.id/warta/nusantara/setelah-nasa-rilis-foto-foto-bukaan-lahan-di-ikn-nusantara (VERIFIED)
- Regulation: Perka OIKN 10/2024 sets WP KIPP delineation at 6,669.64 ha. https://ikn.go.id/storage/regulasi/20241007-perka-oikn-10-2024-perubahan-atas-perka-otorita-ikn-1-2023-rdtr-wp-kipp-ikn.pdf (SNIPPET ONLY, from a search summary; not opened)

Comparability: 6,671 ha is the whole KIPP, 921 ha the first built zone; a satellite box around Sepaku differs in scope. The FWI figure is a NGO estimate for a different, earlier period. SNIPPET ONLY: Bisnis, 19 Nov 2022 (https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20221119/45/1599848/otorita-pembangunan-infrastruktur-ikn-lebih-ngebut-awal-2023) reportedly gives 6,671 ha with 921 ha to be built and a mobilization start "sejak pekan lalu" with no groundbreaking, which would put the start around mid-Nov 2022, not Sept. Bisnis 11 Aug 2022 (title: "Konstruksi Proyek IKN Mundur ke September") suggests PUPR planned Sept 2022. Neither opened (403). The start date is therefore unsettled.

## 4. Danau Sentarum National Park, Kapuas Hulu

- Flooded about 9-10 months a year; long dry season July-September when most of the lake dries leaving river channels and permanent lakes; wet-season water 10-12 m above the dry-season level; park about 132,000 ha. Mongabay Indonesia (Travel), 27 Dec 2012. https://mongabay.co.id/2012/12/27/mongabay-travel-danau-sentarum-danau-pasang-surut-terluas-di-asia-tenggara/ (VERIFIED)
- Lake level fluctuates 3-5 m between wet and long dry season; TNDS set at 132,000 ha by SK Menhutbun 34/Kpts-II/1999 (4 Feb 1999); in prolonged dry season most of the lake is dry. Siti Aisyah et al., Prosiding Pertemuan Ilmiah Masyarakat Limnologi Indonesia 2017 (Puslit Limnologi LIPI). https://karya.brin.go.id/34148/1/Prosiding_2017_Siti%20Aisyah_89-96.pdf (VERIFIED, read via pdftotext)
- 2026 context: BMKG expects El Nino to persist into early 2027 and Aug 2026 to be the widest dry-season peak, with most of Kalimantan peaking in August. BMKG, 10 Jun 2026 (link in case 1) (VERIFIED). Seasia.co, 22 Sep 2026, describes the basin turning to grassland in the dry season but gives no 2026-specific data. https://seasia.co/2026/09/22/lake-sentarum-the-seasonal-lake-that-transforms-into-grassland (VERIFIED)

Comparability: All sources are climatological, not a dated 2026 observation. The 3-5 m (LIPI) and 10-12 m (Mongabay) amplitudes disagree, so cite them as ranges and by source. No 2026 water-level gauge reading or Danau Sentarum fire/drought report was found.

## 5. Demak flood, February 2024 (Wulan embankment, Karanganyar)

- First Wulan breach: two points of the Sungai Wulan tanggul broke at Desa Ketanjung, Karanganyar, beside the market, "sekitar pukul 6 pagi" on Thursday 8 Feb 2024; Pantura Demak-Kudus cut, about 0.5 m on the road, nearly 1 m at the market. Media Indonesia via Metro TV, 8 Feb 2024. https://metrotvnews.com/read/b7WCYq1L-banjir-lumpuhkan-jalur-pantura-demak-kemacetan-mengular-hingga-10-km (VERIFIED)
- Closure: WIKA closed the two breach points between Sun 11 Feb and Wed 14 Feb 2024; water had been entering Ketanjung and Karanganyar. WIKA press release, 17 Feb 2024. https://www.wika.co.id/id/media-dan-informasi/siaran-pers/tanggul-sungai-wulan-jebol-wika-sigap-tambal-dalam-3-hari (VERIFIED)
- Earlier floods elsewhere in Demak began Mon 5 Feb 2024 after several river embankments (Tuntang, Cabean, etc.) broke; BPBD Demak counted 30 villages in 7 kecamatan on 8 Feb night (Wonosalam, Karangtengah, Gajah, Karanganyar, Dempet, Karangawen, Kebonagung); Dinas Pertanian: 1,400 ha rice flooded (preliminary, 9 Feb). Republika/Antara, 9 Feb 2024. https://news.republika.co.id/berita/s8keb5328/1400-hektare-padi-di-demak-tergenang-banjir (VERIFIED)
- Peak extent: 41 villages in 8 kecamatan, 84,270 people affected, 22,860 displaced (from about 19,000) around 13 Feb 2024 per BPBD; 2 breaches still under repair on 13 Feb. Mongabay Indonesia, 19 Feb 2024. https://mongabay.co.id/2024/02/19/demak-banjir-parah-dan-pemilu-susulan-pesan-ekologis-bagi-presiden-baru/amp/ (VERIFIED; its own village counts are internally inconsistent)
- A second, separate Wulan breach occurred about 02.00 WIB on 17 Mar 2024 at the same area. Antara, 17 Mar 2024. https://megapolitan.antaranews.com/berita/282192/puluhan-ribu-warga-demak-kembali-mengungsi-karena-jebolnya-tanggul-sungai-wulan (VERIFIED). BNPB head Suharyanto later confirmed the left Wulan embankment broke "dua kali" and was rebuilt in concrete by Nov 2024 (Antara, 19 Dec 2024). https://jateng.antaranews.com/berita/564082/bnpb-pastikan-perbaikan-tanggul-sungai-wulan-demak-kuat-tahan-banjir (VERIFIED)

Resolving the date conflict: the 3-8 Feb range is explained by two things. The regional flood began 5 Feb (other rivers), and the Wulan/Karanganyar breach was 8 Feb. Sources dating floods to 6-7 Feb (NU Online, 19 Feb) are looser. Those that say "March" refer to the 17 Mar re-breach. Peak impact reporting is around 11-13 Feb.

Comparability: The 1,400 ha is rice fields only, an early partial count (a search summary reported 3,280 ha later; not opened, unconfirmed). No official flood-extent hectare figure was found. A UGM thesis computed 8,307.72 ha of inundated paddy for Feb-Mar 2024 (search snippet only). Demak-wide numbers exceed a Karanganyar-area satellite box.
